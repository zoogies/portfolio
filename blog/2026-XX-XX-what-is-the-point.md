## The days of new / The guts of a thing

Learning in general, using AI as a learning tool

...

Let's think for a second about the website you're reading this on right now. How much do _you_, dear reader, care about the implementation of this website? Does it matter to you if it was "ethically sourced" by human labor and stack overflow searches? Would your opinion change if it was implemented by a human that consulted an LLM for assistance, or if you found out the website was completely vibe-coded in one prompt? What if the human iteratively re-prompted an agent to fine-tune the website to their tastes?

The latter example is certainly an expression of artistic vision, though it doesnt "feel" good inside... at least to me, and maybe to you as well. Is the purpose of a thing it's output, or it's process? The answer probably lies somewhere in the middle.

What about extending this to other sorts of labor and products? If say, we could eradicate all diseases forever by building a metric shit-ton of datacenters and unleashing 1000's of LLM swarms onto discovering proteins that cure everything, is it immoral for us _not_ to do this? Certainly any action we can take to save a human life would be moral and good, at least to some. When it comes to medical practice and immediate quality of life improvements, it's hard to take the mathmetician approach of "not asking for this problem to be solved". Certainly the purpose of medicine is not for its practitioners to enjoy the process.
